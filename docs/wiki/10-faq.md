# FAQ

## General

**Q: What is cross-utilization?**
A: Cross-utilization is when an associate from one team temporarily supports another team's project while remaining on their original team. The Resource Management Ecosystem helps facilitate and track this process.

**Q: Who can I contact for help?**
A: Reach out to your platform administrator or your team manager. For technical issues, check with your IT support team.

**Q: Why do I see different menu options than my colleague?**
A: The platform shows different features based on your role. See the [Roles & Permissions](./03-roles-and-permissions.md) page for details on what each role can access.

## Login & Access

**Q: I forgot my PIN. How do I reset it?**
A: Contact your platform administrator — they can update your credentials from the Admin panel.

**Q: My account is locked. What do I do?**
A: After 3 failed login attempts, accounts are locked for 15 minutes. Wait and try again, or ask an administrator to unlock it.

**Q: I can see the login page but get "Access Denied" after signing in.**
A: Your role may not have permission for the page you're trying to access. You'll be redirected to your appropriate landing page. If you believe this is an error, contact your administrator.

## Matchmaker

**Q: Why does everyone show 7.5 hours available?**
A: This means no time entries have been logged yet. As associates log their hours (or an admin imports utilization data), the available hours will reflect reality.

**Q: I searched for a skill but got no results.**
A: Try relaxing your criteria — lower the minimum proficiency, reduce the minimum hours, or search for fewer skills simultaneously. The matchmaker requires ALL selected skills to be present.

**Q: What does the Match Score percentage mean?**
A: It's a weighted combination of skill match (40%), available bandwidth (35%), and proficiency level (25%). A higher score means a better fit for your criteria.

## Requests

**Q: I saved a draft but can't find it.**
A: Go to **My Requests** and filter by "Draft" status. Drafts expire after 30 days if not submitted.

**Q: My request was rejected. What can I do?**
A: Check the rejection reason provided by the approver. The notification may also include alternate associate suggestions. You can submit a new request with different associates.

**Q: How long does approval take?**
A: Approvers receive a notification immediately. If they don't act within 48 hours, they get a reminder. After 3 reminders (about 5 days total), the request escalates to a Senior Leader.

## Resource Climate

**Q: What do the colors on the calendar mean?**
A: Green = team has good capacity (<60% utilized), Orange = moderate load (60-90%), Red = at or over capacity (>90%).

**Q: Why does the calendar show "0.0h / 0.0h" for some days?**
A: If no team is selected or there are no associates in the filtered view, the bandwidth shows as zero. Try selecting a specific team from the filter.

**Q: Can I see future months?**
A: Yes — use the right arrow (→) next to the month name to navigate forward. Approved future requests will show as reduced bandwidth on those dates.

## Time Logging (Associates)

**Q: Can I log time for yesterday?**
A: Yes — change the date in the form to any past date. However, you can only edit or delete entries from today.

**Q: I made a mistake in a past entry. How do I fix it?**
A: Contact your manager or administrator — they can correct entries through the Efficiency Tracker in the Admin panel.

**Q: What processes should I log time against?**
A: The dropdown shows processes based on your assigned skills. If you don't see the right process, ask your manager to update your skill assignments.
