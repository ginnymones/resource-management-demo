# Overview

## What is the Resource Management Ecosystem?

The Resource Management Ecosystem  is a platform that helps teams find the right people for cross-utilization — matching associates who have available bandwidth and the right skills to projects that need them.

Instead of relying on spreadsheets, Slack messages, or word-of-mouth to figure out who's available and qualified, the platform surfaces this information automatically.

## Who is it for?

The platform is designed for operations organizations of a few hundred up to several thousand people. This repository is a demo populated entirely with fictional data.

**Primary users:**
- **Managers and SMEs** — Find and request associates for their projects
- **Senior Leaders** — Oversee resource allocation across teams
- **Administrators** — Manage the platform configuration and data

**Secondary users:**
- **Associates** — Log their working hours so the system reflects real availability

## What problems does it solve?

| Before | After |
|--------|-------|
| Manually tracking who's available across teams | Automatic bandwidth visibility in one dashboard |
| Asking around to find people with specific skills | Matchmaker search returns qualified candidates instantly |
| Informal resource requests via email/chat | Structured request and approval workflow with tracking |
| No visibility into future workload | Calendar view shows current and projected capacity |
| Scattered roster information | Centralized, searchable organization directory |

## Core Capabilities

1. **Matchmaker** — Search for associates by skills, proficiency level, and available hours. Get ranked results with match scores.

2. **Resource Requests** — Submit formal cross-utilization requests, track their status through approval, and manage drafts.

3. **Resource Climate** — Visualize team workload with a calendar view showing capacity day by day, color-coded for quick assessment.

4. **Roster** — Browse all associates across teams with their skills, levels, locations, and managers.

5. **Time Logging** — Associates log their daily hours, which feeds directly into the bandwidth and capacity calculations.

6. **Approval Workflow** — Requests route to the right manager automatically. Escalation, reminders, and partial approvals are built in.
