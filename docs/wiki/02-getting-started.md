# Getting Started

## Logging In

1. Navigate to the platform URL
2. Enter your email address (your company login, e.g., `yourlogin@example.com`)
3. Enter your PIN (6-8 digits) or passphrase (12+ characters)
4. Click **Sign In**

If you enter the wrong PIN 3 times, your account will be locked for 15 minutes. Contact your administrator if you need an immediate unlock.

## First-Time Users

Your account is created by an administrator. They'll provide you with:
- Your email address (used as your login)
- A PIN to sign in with
- Your assigned role

If you don't have an account yet, reach out to your manager or platform administrator.

## Where You Land After Login

Depending on your role, you'll see different views:

| Role | Landing Page |
|------|-------------|
| Associate | Time Logging Dashboard |
| SME / POC | Resource Climate |
| Manager | Resource Climate |
| Senior Leader | Resource Climate |
| Admin | Resource Climate |

## Navigation

The sidebar (or hamburger menu on mobile) shows the pages available to you:

- **Resource Climate** — Workload calendar and table
- **Roster** — Organization directory
- **Matchmaker** — Search for available associates (SME+ only)
- **My Requests** — Your submitted requests (SME+ only)
- **Admin** — Platform configuration (Admin only)

## Dark Mode

Click the sun/moon icon in the top-right to toggle between light and dark themes. Your preference is saved automatically.

## Signing Out

Click your avatar in the top-right corner → **Sign out**.

Your session automatically expires after 30 minutes of inactivity.
