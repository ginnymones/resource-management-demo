# Resource Climate

The Resource Climate page gives you a visual overview of team workload — who's busy, who has capacity, and where bottlenecks are forming.

*Available to: SME, POC, Manager, Senior Leader, Admin*

## Views

### Calendar View (Default)

A monthly calendar grid where each day is color-coded based on team capacity:

| Color | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| 🟢 Green | Available — less than 60% utilized |
| 🟠 Orange | Moderate — 60-90% utilized |
| 🔴 Red | At capacity — over 90% utilized |

Each day cell shows:
- The utilized hours vs. total bandwidth
- Number of active resource commitments (if any)

**Navigation:**
- Use the ← and → arrows to move between months
- Today's date is highlighted with a ring

**View toggles (Manager+ only):**
- **Team View** (default) — Aggregate capacity for the entire team
- **Manager View** — Filter by a specific manager's direct reports. A dropdown appears to select the manager.
- **Associate View** — View a single associate's calendar

### Table View

A sortable table showing all associates with:
- Name
- Team
- Utilized hours
- Available bandwidth
- Capacity status (color-coded)

Click any row to navigate to that associate's full profile.

## Filters

- **Team** — Filter to a specific team/program
- **Process** — Filter by process assignment
- **Time Granularity** (table view) — Daily, weekly, or monthly aggregation

## Associate Profile

Click an associate's name (from the table or roster) to see their full profile:
- Current bandwidth
- Skills with proficiency levels
- Training history (sorted most recent first)
- Success indicators (how many times deployed per process)
