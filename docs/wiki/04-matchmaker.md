# Matchmaker

The Matchmaker helps you find associates who have the skills you need and the available hours to take on additional work.

*Available to: POC, Manager, Senior Leader, Admin*

## How to Search

1. Go to **Matchmaker** from the sidebar
2. **Select Required Skills** — Click the skills dropdown and choose one or more skills you need (e.g., "STL", "Video", "F2")
3. **Set Minimum Proficiency** — Choose the lowest acceptable proficiency level:
   - **Expert** — Most experienced
   - **Proficient** — Solid competency
   - **Trained** — Basic capability
4. **Set Minimum Available Hours** — How many hours per day you need this person (e.g., 2 hours)
5. Click **Search Associates**

## Understanding Results

Results are ranked by **Match Score** (0-100%), which considers:
- How many of your required skills the associate has (40% weight)
- How much available bandwidth they have (35% weight)
- Their proficiency level in the matched skills (25% weight)

Each result card shows:
- **Name and login ID** — Who they are
- **Team** — Which program they belong to
- **Reports to** — Their direct supervisor
- **Match Score** — How well they fit your criteria
- **Available Hours** — How much bandwidth they have today
- **Skills** — Their relevant skills with proficiency badges

## Requesting Associates

1. Select one or more associates by clicking their cards (or the checkbox)
2. A **"Request Cross-Utilization"** button appears at the bottom
3. Click it to proceed to the request intake form

## Pending Approvals Panel

If you're a Manager, Senior Leader, or Admin, you'll see a **Pending Approvals** panel on the right side of the Matchmaker page. This shows requests waiting for your action, with quick links to view details, approve, or reject.

## Tips

- If you get no results, try relaxing your criteria: lower the proficiency requirement, reduce the minimum hours, or select fewer skills
- The matchmaker searches across all teams — you're not limited to your own
- Bandwidth is calculated from the hours associates have logged — if no time has been logged yet, everyone shows full availability (7.5 hours)
