# Associate Time Logging

Associates use the platform to log their working hours. This data feeds directly into the Resource Climate and Matchmaker calculations — it's how the system knows who has available bandwidth.

*Available to: Associate role only*

## Your Dashboard

When you log in as an Associate, you'll see your personal dashboard with three sections:

### 1. Log Time

A simple form to record your hours:

1. **Select Process** — Choose the process you worked on (your assigned processes are shown based on your skills)
2. **Date** — Defaults to today, but you can select a different date
3. **Hours** — Select from 0.25 to 7.5 in 15-minute increments
4. **Notes** (optional) — Add context about what you worked on

Click **Log Time** to submit. A confirmation message appears when successful.

### 2. Today's Entries

Shows everything you've logged today. For each entry you can see:
- Process name
- Hours logged
- Notes (if any)
- Time submitted

**Editing:** Click the pencil icon to modify an entry's hours or notes. Only today's entries can be edited.

**Deleting:** Click the trash icon to remove an entry. Only today's entries can be deleted.

### 3. Timesheet History

A table of all your past time entries with:
- Date
- Process
- Hours
- Notes

**Filtering:**
- Last 7 days
- Last 30 days
- Custom date range (pick start and end dates)

The history is paginated — use Previous/Next to browse older entries.

## Important Notes

- Your logged hours are what the system uses to calculate your available bandwidth
- If you log 5 hours total for a day, the system shows you have 2.5 hours available (out of 7.5)
- Logging accurately helps managers make better resourcing decisions
- You can only edit or delete entries from **today** — past entries are locked

## What Happens With Your Data

Your time entries are aggregated into:
- **Your bandwidth** — How much capacity you have available
- **Team utilization** — Shown in the Resource Climate calendar (visible to managers and above)
- **Matchmaker results** — When someone searches for associates with your skills, your availability is based on your logged hours
